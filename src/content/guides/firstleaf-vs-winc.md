**Short answer:** take **Winc** if you want to choose your own bottles and a price per bottle you can budget (~$16–20, shipping included). Take **Firstleaf** if you want to be surprised and want the cheapest possible test ($44.95 for six bottles, ~$7.50 each) — and accept that the price roughly doubles after the first box.

Both are legitimate clubs. Neither is a scam. They fail in different ways, and the difference is who picks the wine and what the second shipment costs.

## The comparison, on the paperwork

| | **Firstleaf** | **Winc** |
|---|---|---|
| First box | $44.95 for 6 bottles (~$7.50 each) | $100 off spread over your first 4 orders |
| After the intro | ~$16.50–26.50 a bottle, depending on plan, **plus shipping** | ~$16–20 a bottle at member pricing, **free shipping** |
| Who picks | Their algorithm, from your taste quiz — you don't pick | You pick, with a 20% member discount on every bottle and pack |
| Commitment | None per their site | None per their terms |
| Cancel route | Self-service in account settings | Self-service skip/pause/cancel |
| The catch | A **$25 restocking fee** on orders cancelled after they process; credit expires if you cancel instead of pausing | Notice must be at least **3 days before a shipment** goes out |
| Try before joining | No | Yes — their packs are shopable without a membership |

Every figure comes from the clubs' own sites and help centres, last checked **October 17 and 22, 2026**. We have not bought from either — that's in both reviews, and it's why we quote their terms instead of describing a delivery we never received.

## Where the two actually differ

**1. Control.** This is the real fork in the road. Winc is a shop with a member discount: you browse, you choose, you swap bottles before a shipment. Firstleaf is a subscription that learns: you rate wines, and the algorithm decides what arrives. If you won't rate bottles, Firstleaf stays a lottery — that's not a flaw in their service, it's the product working as designed.

**2. The second shipment.** Winc's price is stable and predictable — ~$16–20 with shipping included. Firstleaf's intro is the cheapest entry in our whole set, but the ongoing price is roughly double the intro, and shipping is on top. The number to compare is not $7.50 against $20; it's ~$16.50–26.50 **plus shipping** against ~$16–20 **delivered**.

**3. How you leave.** Both are self-service, which puts them in the better half of the nine clubs we've read. Winc's catch is timing: miss the 3-day window and the box ships. Firstleaf's catch is money: cancel after an order is processed and the help centre documents a $25 restocking fee, and any credit you hold expires when you cancel rather than pausing.

## Which one to pick

- **You want to choose and you want to know the price:** [Winc](/wine-clubs/winc/). A steady ~$16–20 a bottle, free shipping, swap before it ships. Least surprising of the two.
- **You want the cheapest way to find out whether a club suits you:** [Firstleaf](/wine-clubs/firstleaf/). $44.95 for six bottles is a genuinely cheap test. Decide again at the second box, when you're paying real money.
- **You already know you won't rate wines:** skip Firstleaf. The quiz is the mechanism that makes it work.
- **You hate both ideas and just want deals:** our [deals page](/deals/) lists current club offers with the date we checked them.

## The one check that beats this whole page

Find the **second shipment price** and the **cancel button** on the club's own site before you pay anything. If either is missing from their public pages, that's your answer — and it's the same test we apply on [how we test a wine club](/how-we-test/).
