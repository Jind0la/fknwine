A first wine club is usually an impulse: a $40 intro box, six bottles, a nice photo. The decision that actually matters happens three months later, when the second shipment arrives at full price and you find out what you signed up for.

You can settle that in five minutes, before you enter a card. Five questions, in this order.

## 1. What does the second shipment cost?

Not the intro. The second one. Every club we've reviewed publishes an intro price; far fewer make the ongoing price obvious on the same page. If you can't find it in two minutes, that's a signal.

[Firstleaf](/wine-clubs/firstleaf/), for example, sells its first box at $44.95 for six bottles — about $7.50 each — and then moves to roughly $16.50–26.50 a bottle depending on the plan, plus shipping. The intro is real. It's also not the price.

## 2. Can you cancel without talking to a human?

Two of the six clubs we've read put cancellation behind an email or a phone call. The rest let you do it in your account. Neither is illegal — it's friction, and friction is a business model.

Find the cancel path *before* you find the buy button. Our [cancellation guide](/guides/how-to-cancel-a-wine-club/) ranks all six from a button in your account to picking up the phone.

## 3. Is there a minimum commitment?

"Cancel anytime" and "cancel anytime after your first shipment" are different sentences. Gold Medal's terms say the second one. Firstleaf's site says no commitment, but its help centre says orders cancelled after they process carry a **$25 restocking fee**, and any credit you hold expires when you cancel instead of pausing.

Read the help centre, not the landing page. The landing page is marketing; the help centre is the contract in plain clothes.

## 4. Do they ship to your state?

Wine shipping in the US is regulated state by state. A club can be excellent and still not deliver to you. Check at checkout with a real address before you get attached to the plan.

## Monthly, or by the shipment?

Two billing models hide behind the same "wine club" label, and the difference decides how much you spend in a year:

- **A monthly wine club** charges you every month whether or not you want wine that month. [Naked Wines](/wine-clubs/naked-wines/) works this way: $40 a month goes into your wine account, and you spend it when you like (up to 60% off Angel-only prices). It's the cheapest per bottle *if* you keep using it — and dead money if you forget it for three months.
- **Per-shipment clubs** charge when a box goes out, at a cadence you pick: The California Wine Club and Gold Medal start at $49 per shipment, Wine Access runs four shipments a year, Firstleaf ships every 2–12 weeks depending on your plan. You can usually skip a shipment.

If your first instinct is "I'll deal with it later", take the per-shipment model. A monthly wine club rewards attention and punishes autopilot.

## 5. Do you actually want someone else choosing your wine?

This is the question none of the terms answer. Some clubs (Firstleaf) pick for you based on a taste quiz — that's the product, and if you won't rate bottles it stays a lottery. Others let you choose from a list each month. Neither is better; they suit different people.

If you want to choose, the club has to let you choose. If you want to be surprised, say so to yourself before you pay.

## A reasonable first year, honestly

- **Cheapest way to find out if a club suits you:** the smallest intro box, one club, no annual plan. [Firstleaf](/wine-clubs/firstleaf/) at $44.95 for six bottles is the lowest entry we've found among the six.
- **If you want a fixed per-bottle number you can budget:** [Winc](/wine-clubs/winc/) at ~$16–20, or [Wine Access](/wine-clubs/wine-access/) Discovery at ~$25 with the cleanest cancellation terms in our set.
- **If you're buying a gift:** [Gold Medal Wine Club](/wine-clubs/gold-medal-wine-club/) is built for that, and shipping is extra.
- **If you'll spend $40 a month anyway:** [Naked Wines](/wine-clubs/naked-wines/) can be the cheapest per bottle — and pointless if you won't.

The full comparison, including the exact terms behind each line, is on [our wine club reviews page](/wine-clubs/).

## The one rule

**Screenshot the terms before you join.** Intro price, second-shipment price, cancel path, restocking fee. If a support agent later tells you something different, you'll have the page you read. We do this ourselves, and it's the reason our reviews quote the help centre instead of the sales page.

We have not bought from these clubs — we read their paperwork and say so on every review, with the date we last checked it. Where a number is the club's own claim, we mark it as theirs.
