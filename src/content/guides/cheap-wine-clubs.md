Every "cheap wine club" pitch on the internet quotes the same number: the intro box. Six bottles for $44.95, twelve for $79.99, "$8 a bottle!" — and then the second shipment arrives at a price nobody mentioned.

So here are all nine clubs, ranked by the number that actually matters: **what you pay per bottle once the intro is over.** All of it comes from the clubs' own sites and help centres, last checked **October 22, 2026**. We quote the clubs' claims and say where we haven't tested them ourselves.

## The three numbers most "cheap" lists hide

**1. Shipping is not in the per-bottle price.** The California Wine Club and Gold Medal both publish a per-bottle figure that excludes delivery. On a two-bottle shipment, shipping can be a meaningful slice of what you actually paid. Firstleaf's ~$16.50 excludes shipping too — its own help centre says "$99 **plus shipping**".

**2. Intro pricing is a customer-acquisition cost, and it ends.** Firstleaf's $7.50 a bottle is real and we don't knock it — it's a genuinely cheap way to test a club. But it's the first box. The ongoing price is roughly double.

**3. A "free" or cheap first box can come with a restocking fee.** Firstleaf's help centre documents a **$25 restocking fee** on orders cancelled after they process, and any credit you hold expires when you cancel rather than pausing. That's not on the offer page.

## What "cheap" should mean to you

If you want the lowest **entry** cost: [Firstleaf](/wine-clubs/firstleaf/) at $44.95 for six bottles. Accept that you're buying a test, not a subscription you've chosen.

If you want the lowest **steady-state** cost: [Winc](/wine-clubs/winc/) at ~$16–20, or [Naked Wines](/wine-clubs/naked-wines/) if you'll commit $40 a month and use it.

If you want the lowest cost **per quality bottle** and don't mind $25+: [Wine Access](/wine-clubs/wine-access/) Discovery, whose terms and cancellation are the cleanest we've read.

## The check that beats every ranking

Before you pay anything, find two things on the club's own site: the **price of the second shipment** and the **cancel button**. If either is missing from their public pages, that's your answer — not the intro price.

We haven't bought from these clubs. What we can verify, we verify against their own paperwork, and every review carries the date we last checked it. Where a claim comes from the club rather than from us, the review says so.

That's the whole trick to a cheap wine club: the price you'll pay in month four, not the one in the ad.
