**Short answer:** **Naked Wines** is for people who will spend $40 every month and want to choose from a list at member prices — it can be the cheapest per bottle you'll find, and it's dead money if you forget it. **Firstleaf** is for people who want a cheap, one-off test and are happy to let an algorithm pick — its $44.95 first box is the lowest entry in our set, and its ongoing price is roughly double.

They're not two versions of the same thing. One is a prepaid fund, the other is a quiz-driven subscription.

## On the paperwork

| | **Naked Wines** | **Firstleaf** |
|---|---|---|
| What you pay | $40/month into **your own wine account** | $44.95 for your first box of 6 bottles |
| What that buys | Anything in their shop at Angel prices, up to 60% off | The box they picked, ~$7.50 a bottle |
| After the intro | Same member prices, every day — no end date | ~$16.50–26.50 a bottle depending on plan, **plus shipping** |
| Who picks | You, from their range | Their algorithm, from your taste quiz |
| Commitment | No tie-in per their terms | None per their site |
| Cancel | Phone **1-800-673-4718** or email **chat@nakedwines.com** — we found no published self-service button | Self-service in account settings |
| The catch | The rhythm: $40 a month is only worth it if you actually spend it | A **$25 restocking fee** if you cancel after an order processes; credit expires on cancel |
| Shipping | Not to DE, NJ, RI, UT or dry areas | Most US states, extra cost |

Checked against both clubs' own sites and help centres on **October 16 and 22, 2026**. We haven't bought from either; every number here is quoted from their paperwork, not from a delivery we received.

## The difference that decides it

**Naked Wines is a wallet. Firstleaf is a subscription.**

With Naked, the $40 isn't a fee — it lands in your balance and you spend it whenever you want, on whatever you want, at prices that are permanently discounted for members. That's genuinely the best per-bottle economics in our six clubs *if* you keep using it. The failure mode is silence: three months of autopilot is $120 sitting in an account you forgot about.

With Firstleaf, you're buying a series of boxes and the algorithm decides the contents. The intro is cheap enough to be a real test. The second box is where you find out if the model suits you — and by then you're at roughly supermarket-plus prices with shipping on top.

**The cancel route matters more here than anywhere else in our set.** Firstleaf lets you click your way out. Naked Wines makes you call or write — not sinister, just friction, and friction is a business model (we cover it in [how to cancel a wine club](/guides/how-to-cancel-a-wine-club/)).

## Which one

- **You already spend $40+ a month on wine and like choosing:** [Naked Wines](/wine-clubs/naked-wines/). Best steady-state price of the six, if you keep it fed.
- **You want to test a club once, cheaply:** [Firstleaf](/wine-clubs/firstleaf/). $44.95 for six bottles, then decide at the second box.
- **You hate phone calls more than you hate paying more:** Firstleaf. The exit is a click.
- **You're price-shopping across all six:** [cheap wine clubs, ranked by real cost per bottle](/guides/cheap-wine-clubs/) puts every club on the same line.

## The check that beats the comparison

Whatever you pick, find two things on the club's own pages before paying: the **price of the second shipment** and the **cancel route**. Both of these clubs publish them — and both bury one of the two.
