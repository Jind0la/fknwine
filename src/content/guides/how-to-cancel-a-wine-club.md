"Cancel anytime" is on every wine club's landing page. What's *not* on the landing page is the part that decides whether cancelling is a ten-second click or a phone call on hold. That difference is the whole game — and it's the first thing we read before we ever quote a club's price.

Here's the cancellation reality for the clubs we've reviewed, as of **October 16, 2026**. All of it comes from the clubs' own sites, and we say which ones we've actually tested (spoiler: none of them yet — we tell you that, too).

## The cancellation ladder (easiest → hardest)

| Club | How you cancel | Our read |
|---|---|---|
| **Wine Access** | Account → Account Settings → **"Skip or Cancel"** | Self-service button. The cleanest path we've found. |
| **Winc** | Skip, pause or cancel in account settings (their claim) | Self-service, per their site. |
| **The California Wine Club** | "Make Changes to My Existing Membership" in your account, or phone/email | Self-service exists, per their FAQ. |
| **Firstleaf** | Account settings — cancel or reschedule between shipments (their claim) | Self-service, per their site. |
| **Naked Wines** | Phone **1-800-673-4718** or email **chat@nakedwines.com** | No published self-service button — you contact them. |
| **Gold Medal Wine Club** | Account page, email **support@goldmedalwine.com**, or phone **1-800-266-8888** | A real account option *plus* human routes. |

Read that column again: two of the six put cancellation behind a phone call or email. That's not a scam — it's just friction, and friction is the point. A club that needs you to call is betting some of you won't. A club that lets you click has decided your exit isn't worth slowing down.

## The rule we apply to our own money

**Find the cancel button before you find the buy button.** Twenty seconds on the terms page tells you which kind of club you're joining. If you can't find how to leave, assume the leaving is the hard part.

## The three fine-print traps that actually cost money

1. **"After your first shipment."** Gold Medal's terms say it plainly: *"modify or cancel your subscription anytime after your first shipment."* That first box is the commitment. Several clubs bury exactly this.
2. **The shipment already in motion.** Every club draws a line: cancel before it, and you're out; cancel after the box ships, and it arrives anyway (and you paid). Find that line *before* you sign up, not while you're trying to leave.
3. **Credit vs. cash.** The "guarantee" on most clubs is account credit, not money back. Leaving doesn't refund a credit you never spent — check what happens to any balance when you cancel.

## The pre-join checklist (screenshot it, settle every argument later)

1. **Where is the cancel button?** Account page, email, or phone?
2. **What's the minimum?** Is there a first-shipment commitment?
3. **What happens to a shipment in transit?**
4. **Credit or cash on the guarantee?**
5. **Can you pause instead of leaving?** (Skipping a month is often all you wanted.)

If any of those five has no answer on the club's site, that's your answer: they'd rather you found out later.

## What we're honest about

We have **not** run these cancellation flows ourselves yet — the "how" columns are the clubs' own published terms, quoted, with the date attached. When we test the flows, each club's review gets updated and the date changes. Until then, treat this as the map, not the drive: the button is where the club says it is, and the day you test it is the day you find out how smooth it really is.
