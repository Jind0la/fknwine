**What it is:** a traditional wine-of-the-month club with three levels. **Gold, Platinum and Diamond** send curated bottles from small-production California wineries (with the occasional international guest) plus printed tasting notes. You configure bottle count and cadence at signup, and it works like any good subscription: no contract, cancel whenever — after the first box.

## The math (the only part that matters)

| Tier | From (per shipment) | Shipment contents | Cadence |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Gold** | **$49** | 4 featured wines (2 reds, 2 whites) | monthly / bi-monthly / quarterly |
| **Platinum** | **$114** | 3 unique reds, 90+ rated | monthly / bi-monthly / quarterly |
| **Diamond** | **$220** | 3 unique reds, 92+ rated | **quarterly only** |

- **Per bottle at the base:** Gold at 2 bottles for $49 is **~$24.50** — before shipping. That's liquor-store-plus money, not supermarket money.
- **Shipping is the same for 2, 4 or 6 bottles** and **free at 12** — so the more bottles per box, the less shipping drags on your per-bottle cost.
- **Prices scale with bottle count and tier** — the product page recalculates as you configure. The number above is the floor, not the whole story.

The math to run: **is $25+ a bottle a normal buy for you, or a special-occasion one?** If it's normal and you like someone else picking small-producer bottles, Gold is a clean, no-contract way in. If you're hunting the cheapest glass, this isn't the club — that's the supermarket's job.

## The fine print we read

- **"No obligation, modify or cancel your subscription anytime after your first shipment."** The first box is the commitment; after that you're free.
- **Recurring billing, stated up front.** "By submitting your order for an Ongoing Membership you authorize us to process the recurring charge plus shipping … until you cancel."
- **Shipping is billed separately** and varies by destination and bottle count — it's not baked into the headline price.
- **21+ signature required.** If the carrier has to reship, that's **$40**. Shipments leave 2 business days after ordering; deliveries take **3–7 business days** (TX and MI can take up to 15).
- **Weather holds.** They reserve the right to hold a shipment in bad conditions — annoying in the moment, honest about the wine.
- **Funds back in 3 business days** if you cancel with a credit balance and nothing already in transit.

## Who it's for

- You want curated small-producer bottles without doing the research, at a spend you set yourself.
- You drink enough that a monthly-or-quarterly box makes sense, and you value flexibility over a locked contract.
- You're buying a wine gift that reads traditional and considered — this is one of the category's oldest, most gift-shaped clubs.

## Who it's not for

- You optimize hard for price per bottle — shipping pushes the landed cost up.
- You want to hand-pick every bottle (curation is the product here).
- You're in AL, AR, DE, MS, SD or UT, or you can't take a signature delivery.
