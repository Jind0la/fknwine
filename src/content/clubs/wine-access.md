**What it is:** a curated wine club from the retailer Wine Access, which tastes a claimed ~20,000 wines a year and puts its favourites into three clubs. Think of it as being on the mailing list of wineries you'd never otherwise get on — with tasting cards and video tastings thrown in. **Discovery** is the everyday tier; **Connoisseurs** and **Collector's** go upmarket fast.

## The math (the only part that matters)

| Club | Per shipment | Bottles | Per bottle | Cadence |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Discovery** | **$150** | 6 | **~$25** | 4×/year |
| **Connoisseurs** | **$150** | 2 | **~$75** | 4×/year |
| **Collector's** | **$250–400** | 1 | **$250–400** | 4×/year |

- **All members get:** free club shipping, **10% off all Wine Access purchases**, tasting and pairing cards, exclusive video tastings, and the ability to add wines to a shipment.
- **Free shipping site-wide over $150** — so a non-club order can also ship free once it clears that.
- **Cancel or pause is self-service** in Account Settings ("Skip or Cancel").

The math to run: **Discovery is the only tier where "per bottle" means what it says — $25.** Connoisseurs and Collector's are a different game: you're paying for access and provenance, and the per-bottle number stops being the point. If your budget is real-weeknight wine, stick to Discovery or shop the store. If you're building a cellar of allocated bottles, the top tiers exist for exactly that.

## The fine print we read

- **"No, you can cancel anytime"** — their words, and the mechanism is a button in Account Settings, not a phone call.
- **You can make it a one-off.** Their FAQ notes you can order a single club shipment by cancelling after the first one, or by gifting a single shipment.
- **State rules apply.** Wine shipping is state-regulated; they'll tell you at checkout whether they reach you.
- **The guarantee is credit-based** — "a credit for any bottle that fails to impress," not a cash refund.
- **Gift memberships exist** for any club if you'd rather send it than drink it.

## Who it's for

- You want curated bottles with a known editor's eye behind them, and free shipping on the deal.
- You're happy at Discovery's ~$25 a bottle and want to try a classier box than the quiz-driven clubs.
- You're building a collection and want access to allocations you can't get at retail.

## Who it's not for

- You want the cheapest possible bottle — Discovery is good, but it isn't cheap.
- You need to cross-shop labels at your local store — much of the catalogue lives only here.
- You're on a tight monthly budget and the top tiers would tempt you past it.
