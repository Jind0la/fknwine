**What it is:** the least club-like club on this site. Wine Insiders is a Shopify shop that also sells a quarterly case. You browse a catalogue of a few hundred bottles — France, Spain, Portugal, Chile, South Africa — at $13.99–$24.99, and buying 6, 12 or 15 bottles takes 35%, 45% or 50% off. The membership exists mainly to sell you the intro case.

That's not a criticism. It's a different shape from Winc or Firstleaf, and it changes the question you should ask: not "is the subscription good?" but "is the catalogue any good, and can I leave?"

## The math

| What you pay | Per bottle |
|---|---:|
| 15-bottle intro, $149 | **$9.93** |
| Same case at the listed regular price, $358.85 | $23.92 |
| Single bottles, $13.99–$24.99 | $13.99–$24.99 |
| 6 bottles at 35% off, + $2.95 processing | depends on the bottle |

The intro is the whole pitch, and at $9.93 it's a real discount — not a rounding trick. But two numbers undercut it:

1. **The front page and the product page disagree.** The homepage banner reads "15 Bottles for $99"; the club product page charges **$149.00**. Same case, two prices, live on the same day. Assume $149 until someone on the phone tells you otherwise.
2. **The $358.85 "regular" price is doing the persuading.** Nobody pays that — it's the anchor that makes $149 look like a 58% saving. Judge the intro against what you'd pay for the same wine elsewhere, not against the crossed-out number.

## The fine print we read

- **Shipping:** 3–5 bottles cost **$14.95** plus **$2.95** processing; 6+ bottles ship free plus the same **$2.95**. So there is no such thing as a fee-free order here.
- **Excluded states:** Alaska, Arkansas, Delaware, Hawaii, Mississippi, Rhode Island, South Dakota, Utah. No P.O. boxes.
- **Undeliverable packages:** a **$25 restocking fee** may apply, and shipping fees are not refundable.
- **Damage window:** you must report a lost or damaged shipment **within 60 days** of the expected delivery date, or they accept no liability.
- **3-bottle minimum** — packaging fewer doesn't work for them, they say.

## Cancelling Wine Insiders (the part we couldn't answer)

This is where we have to be blunt: **we found no published cancellation procedure.** Their refund-policy page doesn't state one — it hands you a link to the terms of service. Their `/pages/faq` URL returns a 404 ("This page has been decanted"), and no help-centre article turned up for cancelling the club.

That's not proof there's no exit. It's proof that the exit isn't documented where a customer would look, which is exactly the situation where people end up writing angry reviews.

**Before you join, get one answer in writing:** how do I cancel the quarterly club, and what happens to a shipment already scheduled? If a support reply can't answer that, the intro price is not the deal you think it is.

## Who it's for

- You want a **catalogue** with volume discounts, not a curated subscription — picking your own bottles is the point.
- You'll buy 6+ bottles at a time: that's where the free shipping and the 45–50% tiers live.
- The $149 intro case fits your budget and you're willing to make one phone call later.

## Who it's not for

- You want click-to-cancel. Firstleaf and Plonk both document a self-service route; this one doesn't.
- You live in one of the eight excluded states, or you need a P.O. box.
- You're comparing on "cheapest bottle ever" — a $9.93 intro is a one-time number, not a price.

## What we checked (and what we didn't)

Checked on **October 22, 2026**: the shop catalogue and single-bottle prices, the quarterly club product page ($149.00 vs $358.85), the shipping policy (fees, excluded states, 60-day damage window, $25 restocking), and the refund-policy and FAQ pages.

**We have not bought a case, and we have not tried to cancel** — we can't, because there's no documented route to try. Nothing here is a tasting note; it's what the paperwork says, and where the paperwork is silent we say so.
