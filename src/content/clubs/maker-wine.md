**What it is:** Maker Wine puts still and sparkling wine from small producers into 250 ml cans — a third of a bottle each, so roughly one and a half big glasses. Twenty-plus gold medals, dry with 0 g sugar and under 5 g carbs per serving, and every wine comes from a female or minority-led winery. You can buy single packs (Best Sellers, Women-Owned, sparkling, red, natural, plus individual varietals), the 12-can advent calendar, or join the quarterly **Can Club**.

*Disclosure, plainly: links on this page are affiliate links. If you buy through them, Maker pays us 15 % of the sale, and that is what pays for this kind of price checking. It does not change the verdict, and Maker had no say in the text.*

Run it per glass instead of per bottle, because that is how you actually drink it: a can holds about **1.7 five-ounce glasses**. At the 6-pack price that is **~$5.52 a glass**; in the club it drops to **~$4.78 (12-can tier)** and **~$4.24 (24-can tier)**. A $10 supermarket bottle is **~$2.00 a glass**, a $25 bottle **~$5.00**.

So the honest version: **Maker is priced like a $24–28 bottle** — you are not paying for cheap wine, you are paying for the portion. If the alternative in your fridge is a $10 bottle that you pour once and then forget for three days, the can wins on waste alone. If you finish bottles, it loses on price every time.

## The fine print we read

- **"No commitments, skip or cancel anytime"** — that is their club page, verbatim. Billing is quarterly, not monthly.
- **The tier discount is the whole point of the club**: 10 % off all wines on the 12-can tier, 20 % on the 24-can tier, plus members-only wines and free shipping. At $97 per quarter you are committing ~$388 a year to get there.
- **Watch the pack size when you compare**: the $54–72 prices are for 6-can packs. Per can that is $9.00–12.00 — the single-varietal cans can cost more than the mixed pack, not less.
- **The advent calendar is a presale**: $125 instead of $139, 12 cans, shipping in early November. That is gift pricing, not drinking pricing.
- **US shipping only**, and the state list is not on the pages we read — checkout verifies your address. Alcohol orders need an adult present, same as any wine delivery.
- **The award claim is theirs, not ours**: "highest-rated canned wine ever, with 20+ gold medals". We quote it; we have not verified the medal table.

## Who it's for

- You drink one glass with dinner and hate pouring the rest away.
- You want wine for a picnic, a concert, a hotel room or a boat — where glass is a problem.
- You are buying a gift that looks like it was thought about, at a $56–125 price point.
- You want dry wine with 0 g sugar, not the sweet canned stuff that dominates the shelf.

## Who it's not for

- You optimise for price per bottle. $24–28 a bottle-equivalent is not that game.
- You want to cellar something, or you like a bottle on the table.
- You are outside the US.
- You want to taste before you spend $97 a quarter — start with the 6-pack instead of the club.
