**What it is:** the narrowest, most opinionated club in this catalogue, and it's better for it. Plonk exists to put **organic and biodynamic wines from small producers** in front of you — indigenous grapes, untapped regions (Hungary, Croatia, Greece), low intervention, no added sugar, low sulfites. If that sentence sounds like a marketing list, the difference is structural: the whole catalogue follows it, so you're not choosing between "natural" and "conventional" — every bottle is the former.

It's the only club we've reviewed where the pitch is a *philosophy* rather than a price.

## The math

| Club | Case | Per bottle |
|---|---|---:|
| Mixed / Red / White, monthly | 4 bottles at **$134.99** | **~$33.75** |
| Same, larger cases | 6 or 12 bottles | price scales, per-bottle drops |
| Shipping | **free, always** | $0.00 |

$33.75 is real money — roughly double Winc, and the highest per-bottle number on this site. Whether that's expensive depends entirely on the comparison: against supermarket organic wine at $18, it's a lot; against a natural-wine shop in a city, where $30–$40 for a small-production bottle is normal, it's fair.

One honest note: the wines in the shop list at $29.99 individually, so the club price is not a deep discount — it's a curation fee you're paying for someone else choosing well.

## The fine print we read

- **Billing:** first month at purchase, then on the **1st of each subsequent month**.
- **Prepaid plans** (3, 6, 12 months) are paid in full up front and are **non-refundable final sales**. They do **not** auto-renew.
- **Shipment timing:** all club shipments leave the **first week of each month**; arrive in **5–10 business days**. Buy after the 1st and you're in the following month's shipment.
- **Case composition:** 4- and 6-bottle clubs get that many *different* wines (one bottle each); the 12-bottle club gets **two bottles of six wines**.
- **Address change mid-transit:** $20. Between shipments: free.
- **Reorders** from the shop: 6-bottle minimum, free shipping.

## Cancelling Plonk (the clean one)

The FAQ states it without hedging: **cancel, pause or skip anytime in your account online**, or email **help@plonkwineclub.com**. Prepaid plans simply end and don't renew.

The one rule with teeth: to be excluded from the next shipment and avoid the charge, cancel **up to seven business days before the 1st of the following month**. Not "anytime and it stops instantly" — there's a cut-off, and it's a week and a bit before the billing date.

Compare that to Naked Wines (phone or email, no button) and WSJ Wine (phone only), and Plonk is the easiest club here to leave. **We have not tested the flow** — but it's the only one of the three where the documented route is self-service.

## Who it's for

- You want **organic/biodynamic and small-production** wine and are willing to pay for the farming.
- You like being surprised by grapes you can't pronounce, with tasting notes and recipe pairings attached.
- You'd rather set a monthly rhythm than chase deals.
- You want the exit to be a click, not a phone call.

## Who it's not for

- You're optimising cents per bottle — this is the wrong club, deliberately.
- You want to choose your own bottles; the shop exists, but the club is curated blind.
- You'd consider prepaying 12 months. It's non-refundable, and a year is a long time to be wrong about a grape.
- You need delivery before the first week of next month.
