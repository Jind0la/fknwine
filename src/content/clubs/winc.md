**What it is:** a wine club that quizzes your taste (six questions), matches you with bottles, and refines the picks as you rate what you drink. You can swap bottles before a shipment goes out — three days before, they email you the lineup.

## What happened to Bright Cellars?

If you're here because you searched for Bright Cellars: it's a Winc membership now. Bright Cellars customers were moved over in **October 2024**, accounts transferred automatically, and `brightcellars.com` now sends you to a Winc activation page. Same schedule, similar perks — free shipping, skip anytime, satisfaction guarantee — plus the 20% member discount. Nothing for you to do if you were already a member; if you weren't, everything below applies to you as a new one.

The math to run: **would you pay $16–20 a bottle at the store for wines someone else picked?** If yes, Winc is a convenience play with a strong first year — the $100 intro carries the first four orders, then you're at member pricing. If you're a $8 supermarket drinker, this is not your club — genuinely.

## The fine print we read

- **No commitment, per their site.** Skip, pause, or cancel anytime; swaps before shipping are self-service.
- **Free shipping on subscription orders** — no shipping minimums on member deliveries.
- **The $100 comes over 4 orders, not all at once.** Read that twice. It's still a good deal; it's just not a "free wine" coupon.
- **State rules apply.** Wine shipping is regulated; select your state and see before you fall for a bottle.
- **Someone 21+ signs at the door.** Plan for it.

## Who it's for

- You drink $15–20 bottles, like trying unfamiliar labels, and won't miss picking every bottle yourself.
- You want the "quiz → rate → refine" loop to do the shopping for you.
- You were a Bright Cellars member and want to know what you're in now.

## Who it's not for

- You're loyal to the $8 supermarket shelf. Stay there.
- You want famous-name producers. Winc leans on its own labels.
- You want to hand-pick every single bottle (swaps exist, but the base is curated).
