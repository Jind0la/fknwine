**What it is:** a wine club that quizzes your taste, then ships six bottles it thinks you'll like. You rate each bottle, it refines the next box. Think Spotify's algorithm, but for wine — and like every algorithm, it's only as good as your feedback.

## The math (the only part that matters)

| | Price | Per bottle |
|---|---|---|
| First box (6 bottles) | $44.95 | **~$7.50** |
| Every shipment after | $99 | **~$16.50** |

The intro box is the honest hook: 6 bottles for the price of 3 average ones. The real question is the second shipment. At ~$16.50 a bottle, Firstleaf competes with decent liquor-store bottles — not with your $8 supermarket run. If you drink $15–20 wine and hate shopping for it, the math can work. If you're happy with cheap supermarket bottles, treat it as an experiment — not a new weekly habit.

## The fine print we read

- **No commitment, per their site.** Change, reschedule, or cancel — shipping cadence is adjustable between 2 and 12 weeks.
- **Shipping is state-regulated.** Wine can't legally ship everywhere in the US. Check your state at checkout before you get excited.
- **You need an adult at the door.** Someone 21+ has to sign for delivery. Plan for it.
- **The guarantee:** if you don't love a bottle, they credit you for a different one. Credits, not cash back — read that twice.

## Who it's for

- You're wine-curious, drink 2–5 bottles a month, and think $12–18 a bottle is fair.
- You want variety without reading shelf labels for 20 minutes.
- You'll actually rate the bottles (30 seconds each) so the algorithm gets smarter.

## Who it's not for

- You're loyal to your $8 supermarket bottle. Stay there — genuinely.
- You want to *choose* your wines yourself.
- You hate subscriptions on principle. Fair.
