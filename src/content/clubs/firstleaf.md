**What it is:** a wine club that quizzes your taste, then ships six bottles it thinks you'll like. You rate each bottle, it refines the next box. Think Spotify's algorithm, but for wine — and like every algorithm, it's only as good as your feedback.

## The math (the only part that matters)

There isn't one plan — there are four, all six bottles. Read straight off the membership page (checked Oct 17, 2026):

| Plan | First box | Every shipment after | Per bottle ongoing |
|---|---|---|---|
| Classic | $44.95 (free shipping) | $99 **+ shipping** | **~$16.50** |
| Preferred | $59.95 | $114 + shipping | ~$19.00 |
| Premier | $74.95 | $129 + shipping | ~$21.50 |
| Fine Wine | $104.95 | $159 + shipping | ~$26.50 |

The intro box is the honest hook: 6 bottles for the price of 3 average ones. The real question is the second shipment — and note that "~$16.50 a bottle" is the price *before* shipping. At $16.50 plus delivery, Firstleaf competes with decent liquor-store bottles, not with your $8 supermarket run. If you drink $15–20 wine and hate shopping for it, the math can work. If you're happy with cheap supermarket bottles, treat it as an experiment — not a new weekly habit.

## The fine print we read

- **No commitment, per their site.** Change, reschedule, or cancel — shipping cadence is adjustable between 2 and 12 weeks.
- **Shipping is state-regulated.** Wine can't legally ship everywhere in the US. Check your state at checkout before you get excited.
- **You need an adult at the door.** Someone 21+ has to sign for delivery. Plan for it.
- **The guarantee, in full:** report a bottle you didn't like **within 30 days of delivery** to service@firstleaf.com, with the wine name and why — in most cases they apply a bottle credit that adds a replacement to your next club order. Credits, not cash back.

## What the help centre adds (the part the homepage leaves out)

This is where reading past the landing page pays off. Three things, all in Firstleaf's own help centre:

1. **A $25 restocking fee exists.** The membership page says "no fees or commitments". The help centre says: *"There is a $25 restocking fee for any orders cancelled that are processed and/or shipped."* The fee-free version of cancelling is doing it *before* the order processes.
2. **Your credit dies when you cancel.** *"Credits expire when a membership is cancelled or terminated."* Their own advice is to pause instead of cancelling if you're holding credit.
3. **Pause is the designed exit.** The documented path runs Account → Club Settings → Membership Plan → Manage → *Cancel Membership*, and only there do you get the **Pause** option — pick a date, *Save & Pause*. Their own warning: do it before the next order processes, "to avoid a restocking fee."

You can move a ship date from the Club Orders page while the order hasn't processed. After it has, the site can't change it and you're on the phone (1-800-461-7203, service@firstleaf.com, Mon–Fri 9am–7pm ET, Sat/Sun 9am–6pm ET).

## Who it's for

- You're wine-curious, drink 2–5 bottles a month, and think $12–18 a bottle is fair.
- You want variety without reading shelf labels for 20 minutes.
- You'll actually rate the bottles (30 seconds each) so the algorithm gets smarter.

## Who it's not for

- You're loyal to your $8 supermarket bottle. Stay there — genuinely.
- You want to *choose* your wines yourself.
- You hate subscriptions on principle. Fair.
