**What it is:** a wine club that features a different small California winery every month and hand-picks its wine for members. You choose the format — 2, 4, 6 or 12 bottles, reds, whites or mixed, monthly, every other month or quarterly — and every shipment arrives with tasting notes and pairing suggestions (their *Uncorked* guide). Want only reds, only whites, or an unusual bottle count? Their FAQ says ask, and they'll customize the membership.

## The math (the only part that matters)

| | Number | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Base configuration | **$49.00 per shipment — 2 bottles** | product page (checked Oct 16, 2026) |
| Per bottle at base | **~$24.50** | our math on their price |
| Shipping | Same cost for 2, 4 or 6 bottles — **free at 12 bottles** | their product page |
| Higher tiers | Signature Series **from $134** · Aged Cabernet Series **from $269** | their product pages |
| Cancellation | **Anytime, no fees** | their FAQ |

The math to run: **$49+ per shipment means you're paying for someone's judgment, not for volume.** If $25 a bottle is a special-occasion budget for you and the fun is discovering a tiny winery you've never heard of, this is one of the cleanest clubs in the category. If you want the cheapest glass on the table, this is not your club — that's what the supermarket is for. (And before you subscribe, click your state — wine shipping rules vary, and their checkout makes you verify anyway.)

## The fine print we read

- **"No hidden fees or charges"** on cancellation — their FAQ, verbatim, with a self-service option in the account area.
- **Billing:** billed right before each shipment goes out. Gift memberships are billed up front for the full run.
- **21+ signature required.** Returned packages cost **$40–80** to reship depending on size — their FAQ recommends a business address.
- **Weather delays:** they may hold a shipment in extreme temperatures. Annoying in the moment, honest about the wine.
- **Reorders:** wines from past features can be reordered while supplies last, with member savings — nice if a bottle lands.

## Who it's for

- You like small-producer discovery and don't want to do the research yourself.
- You want flexibility (bottle count, color, cadence) without a contract.
- You're buying a gift that reads thoughtful, not generic.

## Who it's not for

- You optimize for price per bottle. This isn't that club.
- You want brand-name Napa. This is artisan-first, on purpose.
- You can't reliably sign for packages during the week — check the reship fee first.
