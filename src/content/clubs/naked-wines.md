**What it is:** a wine club built backwards from most. Instead of paying per box, you pay **$40 a month into your own wine account** ("the piggy bank"). Naked uses that pool to fund independent winemakers up front, and in return members — "Angels" — buy the resulting bottles at prices they claim are up to 60% off. You choose the wines, or you let Naked tailor shipments to your taste. It's Spotify's model aimed at winemakers instead of musicians, and like any subscription that asks you to prepay, it rewards people who actually drink the wine.

## The math (the only part that matters)

| | Number | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Monthly commitment | **$40 into your wine account** | nakedwines.com (checked Oct 16, 2026) |
| Member saving | **up to 60% off** Angel-only prices | their site |
| Intro offer | **$100 off your first order** (current promo) | their site |
| Shipping (mainland) | Free **over $200**; charged under that | their help pages |
| Intro gift | A **gift bottle each month you order a case** | their site |

The math to run isn't a per-bottle number — it's a **rhythm** question. **Will you spend $40 a month in wine?** If yes, the account is just a wallet with a discount attached, and there's no downside to funding it. If no, you're parking money in someone else's business plan and buying less wine than you bank. Naked isn't hiding this — it's the whole design. Decide whether it fits your drinking, not whether the headline discount sounds big.

## The fine print we read

- **The $40 is a balance, not a fee.** It's yours to spend. But it's also money you've already committed — that's the trade for the Angel price.
- **Shipping is free only over $200.** Under that you pay it. Alaska and Hawaii are $130 and $70 per package. ("Shipments to Alaska and Hawaii have fixed prices" — their FAQ.)
- **Wine shipping is state-regulated.** They can't deliver to Delaware, New Jersey, Rhode Island or Utah, and there are "dry" areas inside other states they can't reach either.
- **21+ signature, three delivery attempts.** After three failed tries, some states destroy the parcel. Call Naked, not the shipper, if it goes sideways.
- **The guarantee is credit, not cash.** A bottle you don't like gets credited back to your account to spend on something else. Read that twice.
- **You don't need to be a member to buy** — but non-members don't get the Angel price.

## Cancelling Naked Wines (what it actually takes)

There is **no published self-service cancel button**. Their help centre documents two routes:

- **Phone:** 1-800-673-4718
- **Email:** chat@nakedwines.com

Their terms state no tie-in — cancel, skip or pause whenever you like. That's the promise; the phone call is the friction. A club that makes you call is betting some people won't, and this is the one place where Naked Wines sits in the weaker half of the nine clubs we've reviewed.

**We have not tested this flow**, so we can't tell you how long it takes or what they ask. Two things to settle before you join, because they're not answered on the landing page: what happens to a **remaining account balance** when you cancel, and whether a shipment already in transit can still be stopped.

If click-to-cancel matters more to you than the per-bottle price, [Firstleaf](/wine-clubs/firstleaf/) and [Winc](/wine-clubs/winc/) both let you leave from your account — we put the difference side by side in [Naked Wines vs Firstleaf](/guides/naked-wines-vs-firstleaf/).

## Who it's for

- You already spend $40+ a month on wine and want a member price on every bottle.
- You like the idea that your money goes to the winemakers, not the middlemen — and you'll notice the wines are exclusive labels.
- You want the option to skip, pause or scale back without a fight.

## Who it's not for

- You buy a bottle here and there. A monthly prepay wallet is the wrong shape for that.
- You want famous, widely-available labels you can cross-shop — most Naked wines exist only here.
- You're in DE, NJ, RI or UT, or you can't take a weekday delivery.
